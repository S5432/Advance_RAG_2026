# Onboarding Notes

## First Week
New hires receive their laptop and badge on day one. IT setup takes about two hours. Attend the HR orientation on Monday at 10:00 AM.

## Probation
The probation period is 3 months. A review meeting is held at the end of month two, and confirmation is decided at month three.

## Learning Budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of 25000 INR for courses and certifications. Approval from your manager is required before purchase.
